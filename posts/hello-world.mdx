# Hello, world

I've been meaning to start this blog for years. Every few months I'd buy the urge off with a "soon" and go back to shipping client work. So here it is: a plain, fast, personal site where I can think in public.

## What you'll find here

Three threads, mostly:

1. **AI and product management.** This is the big one. Generative AI is rewiring how products get discovered, built, and led, and most of the commentary is either hype or dismissal. I'm writing a book on the topic, and this blog is where the early drafts live.
2. **Building digital products.** Craft notes from nearly 20 years of launches: discovery that actually discovers, delivery without theater, and leading product teams inside large organizations.
3. **Games and systems.** I started in game design. Games taught me about feedback loops, economies, and player motivation long before "product-led growth" was a phrase. That lens still shapes how I think about strategy.

## What you won't find here

Hot takes for engagement, growth hacks, or AI-generated filler. If a post doesn't say something I'd defend in a room full of practitioners, it doesn't ship.

If any of that sounds useful, stick around. The first real essay is already up: [what AI actually changes about product management](/posts/ai-and-product-management).
